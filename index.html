About us

Designed around the patient experience.

Sankofa Health Services is an independent healthcare website concept. Our goal for this project is to make service information easier to understand and help people navigate appropriate care options.

Before public launch, the organization, providers, services, service areas and clinical claims must be verified and accurately represented.

Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a real appointment here?

Not yet. This prototype demonstrates the enquiry flow only. Live scheduling requires an authorized provider network and a secure booking platform.

Is virtual care suitable for emergencies?

No. For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. Do not use an online enquiry form for urgent symptoms.

Is this service covered by OHIP?

Coverage depends on the service, provider eligibility and applicable Ontario rules. No coverage or payment claim is made by this prototype.

How will my information be protected?

Before collecting real patient information, the operator must implement appropriate privacy, security, consent, retention and access controls and confirm applicable legal obligations.

If you may be experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 immediately.
